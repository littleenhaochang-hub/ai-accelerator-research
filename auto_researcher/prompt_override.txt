    SPECIFIC RESEARCH FOCUS: DeepSeek Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA) Optimization.
    The baseline code simulates the extreme KV Cache compression of MLA vs standard Multi-Head Attention (MHA). 
    While MLA dramatically shrinks the memory bandwidth bottleneck (e.g., 32MB down to 4MB for Edge GPUs), expanding the latent vector `c_kv` into full Key and Value tensors during the forward pass requires massive O(N^2) FLOPs on the Up-Projection matrices.
    
    Your goal: Propose a novel hardware-software co-design technique to optimize the Up-Projection step of MLA so it does not throttle the ALU (Arithmetic Logic Unit) while preserving the memory savings.
    Consider approaches like:
    1. Low-rank approximations or sparse up-projections.
    2. Fused kernels that compute attention directly in the latent space without full materialization of K and V.
    3. Quantized or block-wise up-projection matrices tailored for Edge NPUs.
    
    The output MUST be a PyTorch simulation that demonstrates a measurable improvement in FLOPs or theoretical latency for the Up-Projection phase compared to the baseline MLA implementation.
